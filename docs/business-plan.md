## 0. The plan on one page

**The goal.** $1,000/month profit means **about $1,075/month of contribution**, after every variable cost (print, shipping, Stripe, AI, discounts, commissions, ad spend) and about $75/month of fixed costs. At our order mix that is **about 45 orders a month: 11 a week, 1.5 a day.**

**Two milestones.**
1. **The first 10 paying strangers by Sat Oct 31**, inside the Founding offer window.
2. **The first $1,000-profit month is December 2026** (base case: about 46 orders, about $1.1K profit). January dips with demand. **$1K every month from February 2027**, carried by partners, referrals and memorial demand, which don't depend on the holidays.

**The engine, in one sentence.** We give away the thing every competitor charges for and makes you wait days to see, a finished portrait of *your* dog, and we put it in front of 100 new dog owners a day by hand until the machine runs without us.

**Three moves, run together so at least one breaks out:**
1. **Gift-first cold outreach, Rule of 100.** Every day, 25 dog creators and 50–75 pet businesses receive a finished portrait of *their own dog*, made in about a minute in `/admin`. Typical portrait shops can't do this: their proofs take 1–3 days.
2. **"We paint your dogs" content.** 3 short videos a day of real photo-to-portrait reveals. Portrait brands are nearly absent from organic TikTok (Crown & Paw: about 11 followers per video after 1,307 videos [S]). The format that wins there is owner emotion, not product montage.
3. **Lead getters that compound.** Creators and partners are paid only on results (15% code for their audience, 15% commission). Customers refer through Give $15 / Get $15. Every Gift Reveal recipient is shown how to make one of their own dog.

**Paid ads amplify; they are never the engine.** We spend only while the cost to acquire a customer (CAC) stays at or below the gross profit that customer brings in within 30 days ($54) [I].

**The commitment.** 100 primary actions a day for 100 days, Thu Oct 15 → Fri Jan 22, 2027. When results come in under target we don't rewrite the plan. We add zeros to the inputs, then fix the worst step in the funnel (§9).

**What it needs from you today.** Revenue is $0 until the AI keys, Stripe and the domain are live. That is 45 minutes, listed in §13.

## 2. The market and the competition

### 2.1 Starving crowd check (Hormozi: pain, purchasing power, easy to target, growing)

- **Pain and desire:** 71M US dog-owning households [S]. Gift demand peaks Nov–Dec. Memorial demand is a steady floor ("pet memorial gift": 9.9–18.1K searches every month [S]).
- **Purchasing power:** hand-painted commissions run $150–500 [S]. Crown & Paw sells at prices close to ours.
- **Easy to target:** breed communities, groomers, daycares, breeders, rescues and dog creators all gather dog owners in one place.
- **The shift:** AI made portraits cheap to make. Trust in them is what's scarce, and our offer (see it first, a person checks it, Love-It Guarantee) is built for that.

### 2.2 How competitors win customers, and where they're weak

| Competitor | How they acquire [S] | Their offer | Weakness we attack |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cuddle Clones** | 545 active Meta ads, about 32 new a week, mostly talking-head testimonials. 1.2M TikTok followers. 31 email-capture forms; its popup asks if the pet has passed away | Plush and gifts, 15–30% off popups | Volume we can't match. We copy the formats honestly: a founder talking head now, real customers later, and a memorial question at capture |
| **Crown & Paw** | The most active on Google: 76 ads in 30 days, 33 of them Shopping. 4.3★ from about 5.3K reviews. "Designed by real artists." Testing ChatGPT ads: Buy 1 Get 1 Free, memorial | 25% off + free $40 mug, BOGO | You pay first and wait 1–3 days for a proof. About 11 TikTok followers per video. Late-Christmas complaints |
| **West & Willow** | Google creative stale (nothing new since Mar 24; some ads point at a cat blog post). Photo-to-portrait and before/after videos. Give $20 / Get $20 referral | Up to 20% off first order. **$10 to preview** | Our preview is free. Their best video formats are ones we can film from our own previews |
| **Gold & Crown** (Italy) | The only brand running "free preview, no card" in US Google ads (since Jul 20); says "powered by AI" | 40% off banner | Same mechanism, but with honest pricing and a named guarantee |
| **Etsy sellers** | About 245K "custom pet portrait" listings, median price about $18. Top listings sell for $3.99–18 with 20–40K lifetime sales. Titles moving this week say "Pet Loss Gift" | Cheap, fast proofs | A price war we shouldn't enter in Q4 (§5.7) |

### 2.3 The gaps we exploit

1. **"See it free first, no card" is almost uncontested in US ads.** It goes in the headline of every ad and the first two seconds of every video.
2. **Organic TikTok is unowned by portrait brands.** Daily reveals, dogs meeting their portraits, and recipients recognizing their own dog.
3. **Incumbents' Google creative is stale.** Tight long-tail search terms with a fresh "free preview" message can win cheap clicks [E, the $40 test checks it].
4. **Everyone discounts, so trust is low** (Trustpilot 3.8–3.9 for the leaders [S]). We lead with the mechanism and the guarantee, never a strikethrough.
5. **Nobody sells instant gifting.** The Gift Reveal from Dec 11 to 25 is creative territory no competitor occupies.
6. **The honest AI middle ground is open.** Competitors either say "real artists only" or "powered by AI, 40% off". We say "made by our AI artist, finished and checked by our team".
7. **Pinterest is neglected.** Every portrait brand has under 1.6K followers there, and gift and memorial planning happens on Pinterest.

**Copied honestly:** mechanism-first search ads, transformation videos, permissioned recipient reactions, a memorial question at capture, gift-with-purchase instead of percent-off, Merchant Center plus Google Customer Reviews, a separate memorial funnel, breed pages, a give/get referral on the Gift Reveal, creator terms that beat the category, and a "Dog of the Month" feature.
**Refused:** timers, permanent sales, BOGO "limited time", competitor names on our site, "hand-painted" claims, gamified popups and any invented social proof.

## 3. The Grand Slam Offer

### 3.1 Value equation scorecard

Value = (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood) ÷ (Time delay × Effort)

| Driver | Our answer (live) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Your dog as a work of art on the wall. The gasp when the gift is opened | Strong |
| **Perceived likelihood** | You see the exact portrait before paying. A person checks every print. The Love-It Guarantee | **Our weakest driver: zero reviews.** Fixed by the first-10-reviews plan (§7.4), never by inventing proof |
| Time delay | Preview in about a minute. Gift Reveal sent the moment you order. Delivered in days | Strongest in the category |
| Effort | One photo. No account. No card to preview. Free US shipping | Strong |

### 3.2 The core offer (live on the site)

- **Attraction:** "Create your free portrait." One photo, about a minute, every style, no account, no card.
- **Stack at the purchase moment** (`web/src/lib/offer.ts`): free preview and unlimited restyles · hi-res digital file included ($29 sold alone) · instant Gift Reveal · checked by a person before printing · free tracked US shipping · the Love-It Guarantee. Only real prices or "Included"; no invented "worth $X" values.
- **Guarantee:** *The Love-It Guarantee.* Tell us within 30 days of delivery and we remake it in any style or refund in full. Every print is checked by a person first.
- **Real urgency:** the Founding offer (15% off our first 100 orders, through Oct 31, applied automatically, real Stripe count). Order by Dec 10 for US Christmas delivery (shown from Nov 1). The Gift Reveal can be sent through Christmas Day.
- **Bundles:** The Heirloom Set (matching ornament + mug, save $10). Order bumps are never pre-checked.

## 4. The money model

Hormozi's order: **attraction** (get cash) → **upsell** (more cash) → **downsell** (save the no) → **continuity** (keep the cash coming).

| Stage | Offer | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Attraction | **Free preview** (the lead magnet: about $0.08 a portrait, about $0.40 per uploader) | Live |
| Attraction | **Founding offer**, 15% off for the first 100 orders through Oct 31, real cap | Live. Never extended or restarted |
| Attraction | **Free Phone Portrait**: your preview as a 1080×1920 lock screen, emailed | Live |
| Attraction | **Buy two portraits, get two matching ornaments free**, Nov 1–Dec 10 (replaces "25% off the second") | Decision §11; build §12 |
| Attraction | **Monthly Portrait Draw**: make a free preview to enter, one framed winner a month, $15 five-day voucher for everyone else | January, after official rules are written |
| Upsell | Anchor ladder (show the largest real piece first, then "Our pick") · prescribe size by wall · second dog +$15 · Diptych $139 · Triptych $199 | Partly live; bundles in §12 |
| Upsell | Bumps: ornament $19, mug $25, Heirloom Set (save $10), never pre-checked | Live |
| Upsell | Thank-you page: "Family Copy", the same portrait shipped to someone else at 20% off, one click with the card on file | Later (needs Stripe card-on-file) |
| Downsell | Nurture emails step down: canvas → framed → fine-art print from $35 → the file alone $29. Same portrait, smaller scope, never a discount on the same item | Next (today's nurture: reminder → what's included → Founding code) |
| Downsell | Test "order in 48 hours and the matching ornament is on us" against a 10% code | Build §12 |
| Continuity | **The Holiday Portrait**: opt in at checkout; each October we make this year's holiday edition of their dog, and they approve it with one tap (never auto-charged) | Q1 build, first harvest Oct 2027 |
| Continuity | Dog's birthday captured at checkout → a free new-style preview each year | Q1 build |
| Continuity | Gift cards (solves "I don't have a photo of their dog") | Q1 build |

**30-day gross profit per buyer: ≈ $54** (≈ $40 during the Founding window), on about $102 of revenue, with conservative take rates [I] (`hormozi-application.md` §B9).

**Client-financed acquisition (CFA)** means a customer pays back what it cost to acquire them within 30 days.

| Level | Rule | Highest CAC we can pay |
|---|---|---|
| CFA | 30-day GP ≥ CAC | **$54** |
| Hormozi's real-life minimum | 30-day GP ≥ 2 × CAC | **$27** |

Cold paid social typically lands at a $37–84 CAC [S]. Paid ads alone can reach CFA, but not 2×. **So at least half of all orders must come from channels paid only on results** (creators, partners, referrals; effective CAC $15–28). That is why the plan is built around lead getters.

## 5. Lead generation: the Core Four, minus warm outreach

### 5.1 Cold outreach A: creators, gift-first (25 a day)

- **Who:** dog accounts with 2K–50K followers on Instagram and TikTok. Choose for views per follower, not follower count. Start with `marketing/creators/prospects.csv` (167 accounts, already in `/admin/partners`). Then add 25 new accounts a day found through breed hashtags (golden retriever, frenchie, doodle, dachshund, lab).
- **How:** `/admin/partners/[id]/gift` → paste their dog's photo → pick the style that fits the dog (big or serious → Royal or Admiral; small or silly → Pop Art or Storybook; senior → Watercolor) → check the likeness → **Copy DM**. The reveal link carries their code, so any order from it is attributed to them.
- **The DM** (full version in `marketing/creators/outreach-playbook.md`): "Made this of [Dog] — thought you'd love it. It's yours, no strings: [reveal link]. If you ever want to share it, your followers get 15% off with your code and you earn 15% on every order."
- **Follow-ups:** day 2 and day 7, one line each. Then stop.
- **Upgrade path:** a creator who shares the digital reveal gets the physical piece (at most 5 a month, ~$45 each, from the reinvestment budget). A creator with 3 or more attributed orders becomes a Partnership/Spark ad, with permission and #ad.
- **Expected [E]:** 20–35% reply, 5–10% share publicly, 1–3% become active affiliates. Platforms throttle new accounts, so stay at 25 a day and personalize every DM.

### 5.2 Cold outreach B: pet businesses, gift-first (50–75 a day)

| Partner type | When | Our offer to them | Expected [E] |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Groomers, daycares, boarding, trainers** | From now; "a holiday portrait with every groom" from Nov 1 | A free portrait of the shop dog, a QR counter card, 15% off for their clients, 15% commission for them | Email: 10–20% reply, 4–8% active. Walk-in: 15–25% active |
| **Breeders** (holiday litters) | Nov–Dec | "The Breeder's Gift": every puppy family gets a free preview and 20% off; the breeder earns 10% | 12–25% reply, 5–10% active |
| **Rescues and shelters** | From now | Free adoption portraits, a 15% adopter code, a flat donation announced in advance (Giving Tuesday, Dec 1). Never "X% of each sale goes to…" (charity co-venturer laws) | 20–35% reply, 10–20% active |
| **Pet crematories, then vets** | January | Remembrance Portrait at reseller pricing, or 15% commission; vets get a clinic-paid Sympathy Portrait | 6–12% reply, 2–5% active |
| **Pet boutiques** | Q1 | A sample on the wall with a QR code, 15–20% commission | 8–15% reply, 3–6% active |

- **The pitch opens with the gift:** the first line of every email is their own dog's portrait. Lists come from Google Maps categories, breed-club directories, Petfinder and the crematory directories (`marketing/partners/b2b-playbook.md`, `b2b-prospects.csv` has 65 to start). Use only emails the business publishes, and log the source.
- **Email at scale needs the domain first** (§13): a mailbox on pupprints.shop, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, a postal address in the footer and a one-line opt-out (CAN-SPAM covers B2B email). Ramp to protect deliverability: **10 a day in week 1, 25 in week 2, 50 in week 3, 75 from week 4.**
- **Walk-ins:** one afternoon a week, 10–15 local groomers and daycares, with QR counter cards (the admin partner card tool, building today) and a phone showing their shop dog's portrait.
- **Target:** 10 active partners by Nov 13, 40 by Dec 31, 50+ by mid-January. Partner orders at 5–10% of all orders by January.

### 5.3 Content: the "We paint your dogs" daily series (3 shorts a day)

- **Platforms:** TikTok first, then the same video to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. 5 Pinterest pins a day (gift ideas, memorial, breed × style). Pinterest is open ground.
- **Formats, ranked by the evidence:**
  1. Screen-recorded live preview: "one phone photo → six portraits in a minute". The speed is the hook.
  2. A dog meeting its own portrait (our dog or a permissioned customer's, labeled honestly).
  3. Gift Reveal reactions from real recipients, with written permission.
  4. "Which style is your [breed]?" Comment A/B/C/D, then reveal the winner.
  5. Founder talking head: "Why we show you the art before you pay." "Don't trust us. Upload one photo and look."
- **The flywheel:** every video ends "Comment your dog's name; I'll paint the next 10." Every commenter gets their portrait by DM (give in public, ask in private). With permission, their dog becomes tomorrow's video.
- **Ratio:** about 4 giving posts to 1 pure ask. Every post can carry the free-preview CTA because it is itself a gift.
- **Hooks to use** (from the 20 in `competitor-acquisition.md` §9): "Phone photo in. Portrait out. Free to see." · "Some love needs a wall." · "For the dog person who has everything." · "Keep them exactly like this." · "It's literally him."
- **Never** claim hand-painted. If asked: "Our AI artist creates it; our team finishes and checks it."

### 5.4 Paid: gated by math

- The **$100 test** is in §6. After that, paid spend comes only from contribution.
- **Scale rule:** raise spend only while the trailing-7-day paid CAC is ≤ $54 (CFA). Prefer ≤ $27. Pause any channel above $54 for 7 days. Kill any ad above $8 per preview after $15 of spend.
- **What we amplify:** only creative that has already won organically (a post at 5× our median) and creator posts as Partnership/Spark ads. Retargeting people who made a preview but didn't buy is the cheapest paid audience we will ever have.

## 7. The operating system: Rule of 100

### 7.1 The daily schedule (6 days a week; Sunday is operations only)

| Time | Block | The actions |
|---|---|---|
| 07:30–09:10 | **Content, 100 minutes** | Film, edit and post 3 shorts (TikTok → Reels → Shorts). Reply to comments. DM every commenter their free portrait. 5 Pinterest pins |
| 09:15–11:15 | **Creator gifts, 25** | Find 25 new dog accounts → generate 25 portraits → check likeness → send 25 DMs. Day-2 and day-7 follow-ups |
| 11:15–12:45 | **Partner gifts, 50–75** (ramp §5.2) | Shop-dog portrait in the first line of each email. Day-3 and day-7 follow-ups. On walk-in day: 10–15 visits instead |
| 13:30–14:30 | **Ads and creative, 60 minutes** (100 once paid is proven) | Turn yesterday's best post into an ad candidate. Write 5 new hooks. Check CAC by channel |
| 14:30–16:30 | **Operations** | Approve every paid order's print file the same day. Remakes. Reply to every message within 2 hours. Send Gift Reveals |
| 16:30–16:45 | **Scorecard** | Log the day in `/admin/scorecard` |

**Minimum dose on a bad day:** 50 reach-outs, 1 short, and operations. Never skip the print check: "every print is checked by a person" is a promise.

**100 days of inputs:** about 7,500 reach-outs (2,500 creators + 5,000 businesses), about 250 shorts, about 1,500 pins.

### 7.2 The weekly rhythm (More → Better → New)

- **Monday, 30 minutes:** find the funnel step with the biggest drop (visit → upload → email → checkout → paid). Pick **one** test for the week. Set the week's ad budget by the reinvestment rule.
- **Wednesday:** walk-in afternoon.
- **Friday:** rank the week's content and promote the top 2 to ad candidates. Call the 10 most engaged partners.
- **More** until Dec 10. **Better** from week 3. **New** only once more and better stop producing, in this order: Spark/Partnership ads → Pinterest ads → Google Shopping → Etsy memorial listings (January).

### 7.3 The scorecard
