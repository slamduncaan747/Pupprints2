# Pup Prints: Market Map and Wedge Analysis for Custom Pet Portraits

*Research date: Oct 12, 2026. Sources: competitor sites, Trustpilot, Grips Intelligence, Similarweb, Google Trends (US, pulled directly), APPA and third-party reviews. Third-party traffic and revenue figures are modeled estimates. Comparison articles written by competitors are flagged.*

---

## 1. Summary

1. **Category demand is growing while the leaders shrink.** US Google searches for "pet portrait" rose about 69% from 2021 to 2025, and "dog portrait" rose about 32%. Over the same years, branded searches for **"crown and paw" fell about 88%** (2021 average 26.9 to 2025 average 3.1, all terms on one scale). In 2021 Crown & Paw's brand searches were larger than the generic category term. In 2025 they were about 1/13th of it. "west and willow" is down about 36% from its 2022 peak. Fewer searchers are asking for a brand by name, so more of the demand is open to a new entrant.
2. **The leaders are small and slow in 2026.** Grips estimates for August 2026:
   - Crown & Paw: about $263K/month, 56K sessions, 2.5–3.0% conversion, $150–175 average order value (AOV), about $3M online sales in 2025.
   - West & Willow: about $398K/month, 61K sessions, 2.5–3.0% conversion, $250–275 AOV, about $3.7M in 2025.
   
   A new brand at about 60K sessions a month would be at their traffic level.
3. **The leaders' ordering process is outdated.** Crown & Paw takes payment first and sends a proof in 1–3 days. West & Willow shows nothing before purchase unless you **pay $10 for a preview**. Both score about 3.8–3.9 on Trustpilot. Their top complaints are holiday deliveries arriving after Christmas, confusion between digital and physical orders, and surprise charges.
4. **AI-native entrants solved the preview problem but nothing else.** Purr & Mutt, Perkie Prints, Gold & Crown, Portrait Union, Pet Canvas and DogArt.ai all show free previews within about a minute. Most are digital-first, cheap ($0.99–$29) and based in the EU or UK, with thin brands, weak physical products and little trust. ChatGPT and Gemini make plain digital output close to free.
5. **No one offers all four of these:** (a) an instant preview that really looks like your dog, (b) a premium framed object made in the US, (c) an artist finish with a likeness guarantee, and (d) gift logistics that never miss the date.
6. **Recommended wedge:** a dog-only brand built on **"See it now. Give it now. Hang it in days."** It combines an instant free preview with an **instant gift reveal** (a digital reveal on the day, with the framed print following). This removes both waits the incumbents impose. It fits Q4 2026 well, because the peak search weeks (Nov 30 to Dec 13) are exactly when the incumbents' production starts to fail.

### Bottom-up estimate (ours)
- 71M US dog households × 2–4% buying a custom portrait product each year (including gifts bought by non-owners) × about $85 average order gives **about $120M–$240M a year for dog-only US portrait sales.**
- Adding cats and personalized pet-face merchandise (socks, mugs, apparel, ornaments) gives a **US total for custom pet art and personalized products of about $300M–$600M.**
- **The market is fragmented.** The two best-known DTC brands each do about $3–4M a year online (Grips, which may undercount app, in-app browser and marketplace sales). Their lifetime claims ("850K+ created", "1M+ sold") imply historical run rates of roughly $8–15M a year, so both have probably fallen from their peaks. Etsy has hundreds of thousands to about 1M "custom pet portrait" listings (third-party estimate). One $9.99 royal portrait listing shows about 21.5K sales and about $215K lifetime revenue (third-party estimate).
- **Serviceable market for Pup Prints** (US, dogs, DTC physical art and gifts): about $200–350M. A realistic year-1 target is $0.5–2M, which would make Pup Prints a top-5 DTC brand in the category.

- **Peak weeks every year: Nov 23 to Dec 20, with the top two weeks being Nov 30 to Dec 13.** These are also the weeks when physical production and shipping is most strained, which explains why holiday delays are the leaders' biggest complaint.
- **Valentine's Day (Feb) and Mother's Day (May)** show clear secondary peaks for "dog mom gifts", "personalized dog gifts" and "custom pet portrait".
- **National Dog Day (Aug 26):** searches for "national dog day" jump 30–50x in that single week (index 80–100 against a baseline of 2–5), but "pet portrait" searches do not move. It is a **social attention moment, not a buying moment**, so it is best used for influencer and UGC launches and giveaways, not search ads.
- **Memorial demand is evergreen:** it stays nearly flat all year with a small Q4 rise (the first holidays without the pet). It steadies revenue between peaks and suits high-AOV products.
- **Anomaly in 2026 data:** Feb–Jun 2026 shows a large surge for "pet portrait" (peak Apr 5–18). The same surge appears in unrelated terms such as "dog bed" (about 6–7x) and "canvas print", so it is **probably a measurement artifact** (a Trends sampling change or automated/agent queries), not real demand. Do not plan inventory around it.
- **Rising related queries in 2026:** "personalized pet portrait" (+500%), "royal pet portrait" (+110%), "custom pet portrait painting" (+120%), "ai pet portrait", "custom pet portrait necklace" (breakout), "pet portrait tattoo" and "pet portrait embroidery". Royal, jewelry and embroidery are the extensions with growing interest.

**Key dates ahead:** Black Friday Nov 27, 2026 · Cyber Monday Nov 30 · physical-shipping cutoff about Dec 14–16 · digital gift reveal can sell through Dec 24 · Valentine's Feb 14, 2027 · National Pet Day Apr 11 · **Mother's Day May 9, 2027** · Father's Day Jun 20 · **National Dog Day Aug 26, 2027**.

## 4. Competitor map

### 4a. Legacy DTC leaders (artist-made or artist-"proofed", no instant preview)

**Crown & Paw** (US, founded 2019, Houston)
- **Positioning:** "One-of-a-kind Custom Pet Portraits… Hand-illustrated." Pets in costume themes (Renaissance, "The General," Film & TV, Sports, Bathroom, Magazine, Floral Crown); 100–150+ designs.
- **Prices:** "From $39.95" on sale. Canvas lists at $59.95–79.95 with constant "Save $40" strikethroughs. Posters $59.95. Each extra pet adds about 20–40%.
- **Products:** A very wide range: canvas 8×10 to 32×48 with frames, posters, blankets, mugs, LED night lights, socks, tees, sweatshirts, "Lifelike Pet Clones" and human portraits.
- **Preview:** **No instant preview.** The artist proof takes "1–2 days" (homepage) or "2–3 days" (how-it-works page), with unlimited revisions. Delivery is about 1–3 weeks door to door.
- **Trust:** 4.8 from 58K reviews on its own site, against **3.8 on Trustpilot (20.2K reviews, 10% one-star, only 33 reviews in the last 12 months)**. Complaints: holiday orders arriving after Christmas or never, customers receiving a digital file instead of the canvas they ordered, shipping protection ("Checkout+") added automatically, inconsistent free-shipping thresholds ($75 vs $100), and slow support.
- **Tactics:** Permanent 25–50% off sales with countdown timers, "$250K+ donated to shelters," a loyalty program, affiliate program on AWIN (60-day cookie; affiliates may not use the brand name in hashtags or handles), Klarna, Shop Pay and Apple Pay, and memorial testimonials.
- **Traffic:** Grips puts sessions at about 56K/month (Aug 2026). Similarweb shows most traffic as direct or organic, with paid search at only about 2.4% of keyword traffic.

**West & Willow** (US, founded 2019, NYC)
- **Positioning:** "The Original Pet Portrait," "1M+ Sold." Minimalist line or illustration style on soft backgrounds, sold framed.
- **Prices:** 8×10 framed $77 (shown as "was $105"), 12×16 $99, 18×24 $154 (walnut/birch +$11). Poster only $46–68. Two pets $121. Desktop $48. Memorial $75. Add-ons: **preview $10**, rush $20, digital file $15.
- **Preview:** **None by default.** The site has said it does "not offer artwork previews or proofs" without the paid add-on. Changes are allowed only within 30 minutes of ordering, and items are typically final sale.
- **Trust:** 4.9 from 22K on its own site, against **3.9 on Trustpilot (19.6K reviews, 13% one-star, 809 in the last 12 months)**. Complaints: delays without status updates, damaged frames, portraits that don't look like the pet, missing digital copies.
- **Tactics:** Free mug added automatically when bundled, "Give $20, Get $20" referral, free shipping over $100. On Meta: 29 active ads with about 9 new creatives a week. Formats are unboxing (15%), before/after (11%), montage, generational split-screen, memorial testimonial and reaction videos. Main hook: "Over 1,000,000 pet parents chose West and Willow." AOV is about $250–275 (Grips), the highest in the category.

**Paw & Glory** (UK, founded 2019)
- **Positioning:** Humor-led costume themes (Peaky Blinders, "Ugly Pet," "Turn Me Yellow") and explicitly **"Created by real artists, not AI."**
- **Prices and process:** From £25.99–33.99. Proof in 2–3 days. **Auto-approves if the customer doesn't reply within 72h.**
- **Tactics:** Cart gift thresholds (free mug at £65, free digital copy at £95, priority queue at £120), a 10-minute cart reservation timer, Klarna and Clearpay, founder story, NYT logo. 2.2K reviews and 30K customers.

**Pop Your Pup** (US, since 2013)
- Pop art; mugs from $28.99, blankets $89.95.
- Proof in 2–3 business days, unlimited revisions, "love it or your money back" before printing. Made in the USA. Claims 200K+ pets.

**My Pet Canvas** (rebranding to Fido & Frame)
- Premium canvas: $127 (12×16) up to $497 (40×40). "No AI filters."
- Preview within 72h. 15K portraits. Lifetime color guarantee on large sizes.

**Hand-painted tier:** Instapainting (from $89–107, 20% deposit, under 3 weeks, 30K commissions, now links an "AI Instant Preview"), PortraitFlip (from $89, 12–30 days), Paint Your Life (from $199, 15–27 days, since 2006), WhataPortrait ($119–209, 2–3 weeks), Splendid Beast (hand-painted oil in costumes, mock-up before painting, discloses a $20–40 tariff fee). These sit at a much higher price, take weeks, and aim at a different buyer.

## 5. What the evidence says buyers want and dislike

1. **They want to see it before paying.** Every newer entrant leads with a free instant preview. The two largest incumbents don't offer one, and one charges $10 for it. This is now basic table stakes.
2. **The gift has to arrive on time.** Holiday delays are the #1 negative theme for Crown & Paw and among the top themes for West & Willow. Demand peaks exactly when fulfillment breaks.
3. **It has to look like their dog.** Poor likeness is the known failure of AI output (smoothed wrinkles, wrong markings, breed features changed) and is also a complaint about West & Willow.
4. **Pricing needs to be honest.** Customers complain about permanent fake sales, auto-added shipping protection, mixed-up free-shipping thresholds, and confusion between digital and physical orders.
5. **Many buyers distrust AI.** Paw & Glory, My Pet Canvas and Instapainting all market "not AI." Research finds that when recipients know AI was involved in a gift, they rate it as less thoughtful and feel less gratitude (Ohio State 2023; Japanese I/O psychology journal 2026, n=567; Savings.com 2025: 36% would question how well the giver knows them). **Implication:** don't lead with "AI." Lead with the outcome and the care ("your dog, perfectly; finished by our artists"), and disclose AI use honestly in the FAQ and on product pages.
6. **The emotional reaction drives sales.** Memorial and "I cried when I opened it" testimonials appear across every competitor. West & Willow's ad mix is built on unboxing, before/after and reaction videos, which is exactly the content influencers produce naturally.

## 6. White space

| Gap | Who leaves it open | What Pup Prints should do |
|---|---|---|
| **Instant preview that looks like your actual dog** | Incumbents have no preview; AI-natives preview fast but with weak likeness | Dog-only model and prompts. Optional 2–3 photo upload. | **Gifts that are never late** | Crown & Paw and West & Willow (Dec failures); AI-natives are mostly digital | **Instant Gift Reveal:** a branded animated reveal page or printable card, sent the moment you buy or scheduled for the day, with the framed piece following. | **A premium physical piece at a mid price** | AI-natives are cheap and digital; hand-painted is $119–$500 and takes weeks | US-printed framed art at $59–199, shipped in 2–4 business days, with simple premium packaging (built for unboxing). |
| **Honest, all-in pricing** | Crown & Paw (fake sales, add-ons), Paw & Glory (auto-approve, timers) | One clear price, shipping included at one threshold, no auto-added protection, digital file included with every physical order. |

## 7. The single best wedge

### "See it now. Give it now. Hang it in days." A dog-only portrait brand with instant preview and an instant gift reveal.

**Why this wedge:**
- It **removes both waits the incumbents impose**:
  - waiting 1–3 days (or paying $10) to see the art;
  - waiting 1–3 weeks to give it, with a real risk it misses Christmas.
- It **turns the category's worst weakness (peak-week fulfillment) into our main selling point.** We can keep selling gifts through **Dec 24** with a digital reveal, while incumbents stop at their shipping cutoffs. Last-minute buyers have the least price sensitivity.
- It **fits every demand peak**: holidays, Valentine's, Mother's Day (dog moms), birthdays, and memorial and sympathy gifts (an instant, gentle gesture).
- It **is easy to film for influencers.** Upload, 30-second preview, reveal, reaction is exactly the format that already sells this category on Meta and TikTok.
- It **holds up against free ChatGPT output**, because the value is the likeness guarantee, curated taste, the framed object and the gifting experience, not the image file.

**Offer architecture (recommended):**
- **Free preview** of 6 curated styles. Launch with fewer, better styles rather than Crown & Paw's 150:
  - Royal / Renaissance (the top-searched and rising style)
  - Minimalist line (West & Willow's look)
  - Watercolor
  - Modern pop
  - Vintage oil
  - Memorial / halo
- **Products:**
  - Framed 8×10: $59–69
  - **Framed 12×16: $99–119 (the hero product)**
  - Heirloom canvas 16×20 or 18×24: $149–199
  - Each extra dog: +$20
- **Gift Reveal and digital file included free** with every physical order. Digital-only at $19–29 exists as a fallback, not the lead offer.
- **Add-ons for AOV:** ornament $24–29 (in Q4), mug, blanket, and later jewelry and embroidery (both rising in search). Target AOV is **$110–140**, against Crown & Paw's $150–175 and West & Willow's $250–275.
- **Margins:** Printful canvas starts at $14.75 base cost, so a 60%+ gross margin is realistic at these prices. Frame costs still need to be confirmed with the chosen print-on-demand partner.
- **Trust layer:**
  - Likeness Guarantee
  - Artist touch-up on every order before printing
  - Published holiday cutoffs
  - No surprise fees
  - Photos deleted after a set period
  - Honest "AI-assisted, artist-finished" disclosure

**Go-to-market notes from the research:**
- **Influencer pricing:** the average pets sponsored post is about $155 (Collabstr 2026). Micro creators (10K–100K followers) charge $250–1,500 per post. TikTok pet creators with 10K–500K followers charge $250–4,500 per video.
- **Influencer approach:** lead with **product seeding** (a free portrait is itself the content), then **15–20% affiliate commission with unique codes**, then Spark Ads or whitelisting of the best videos. Crown & Paw's restrictive affiliate terms leave room to be more creator-friendly.
- **SEO:** incumbents' footprint is modest (Crown & Paw about 12.9K ranking keywords, West & Willow about 6.7K). Build breed × style × occasion pages, including memorial pages.
- **National Dog Day (Aug 26):** use it for giveaways and UGC drives, not search spend.
- **Q4 2026 now:** creator seeding should start immediately so content is live before Black Friday (Nov 27). Search demand peaks Nov 30 to Dec 13. After about Dec 14, push the Gift Reveal message.

**Key risks:**
- Likeness failures. Mitigate with QA, multi-photo upload and the guarantee.
- Q4 print-partner capacity. Lock in SLAs and a backup partner.
- Backlash against AI. Mitigate with outcome-first messaging and honest disclosure.
- Chargebacks from late gifts. The Gift Reveal largely removes this.
- Naming: the searches turned up no "Pup Prints" brand selling dog portraits. A USPTO trademark search is still needed before spending on the brand.
- Don't use competitors' trademarks in ad copy or handles.
