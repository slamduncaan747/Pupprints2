# Gift-First Creator Outreach Playbook

**The play:**
1. Make a portrait of a creator's *own* dog from a photo they posted publicly.
2. Send it to them privately as a gift, with no strings.
3. Let the wow do the work.
4. Offer the affiliate deal only after they react: 15% commission, plus 15% off for their followers through their link.
5. Pay a few nano creators $30 for a guaranteed post.

**We never publish or repost their dog without a clear yes.**

Our cost per gift is about $0.06 and 8 minutes of your time. A paid post from the same person costs $30–300.

Inputs: [prospects.csv](prospects.csv) (166 creators, verified 2026-10-13) · [creator-brief.md](creator-brief.md) (send after a yes) · [partner-agreement.md](partner-agreement.md) · strategy terms in `docs/strategy.md` §8

## 1. Who to gift first (pick order)

1. Filter the CSV to notes starting with **`P1`**, then `P2`. **Skip `LATER-macro` and `P3-mid` this week.** They have managers and weeks of lead time, so they belong in the Q4 pipeline.
2. Before each DM, take a **10-second sanity check** on their profile:
   - They posted in the **last 14 days**.
   - The dog is clearly visible in a recent photo.
   - The bio doesn't say "no brand DMs."
   - The account isn't run by a minor.
   - **No recent loss.** If their latest posts mention a dog passing, stop. Use [template E](#template-e-memorial-variant-extra-gentle) only if the loss is older than about 3 months and they talk about it openly, or skip them.
3. **Mix breeds in every batch.** Frenchies, goldens and doodles are the biggest buyer pools. A rescue pittie or a hairless "bat dog" makes the most striking portrait.
4. **Prefer views per follower over followers.** A 2K account averaging 4K views is worth more than a 40K account averaging 400.

## 3. The 3-touch sequence

| Touch | When | Channel | Template | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | DM, or email if DMs are closed | **A** (gift) | Wow |
| 2 | Day 2 (48h), only if no reply | Same thread | **B** (bump + a second style) | A reason to open |
| 3 | Day 5–6, only if they **opened** the gift (view count > 0) and haven't replied | Email if public; otherwise a genuine comment on their latest post, **not** a pitch | One line: "No reply needed. It's yours to keep 💛" | Goodwill. Then **stop**. |

**Hard rules:**
- **Never more than 3 touches.**
- **Never** DM them on a second platform the same day.
- **"No" or no reply after touch 3 = done.** Mark them `declined` or leave them as `contacted` and don't follow up again this quarter.

Swap the **[brackets]**. Keep them short. Voice: warm, witty, confident.

> Hi [Name]! We're Pup Prints, a tiny new portrait studio, and we're obsessed with [Dog]. So we made [him/her] a little something 🎨
>

>

>
> Want in? I'll send your link + a one-page brief. If you post about us, just tag it #ad or "Gifted by @pupprints" so we're all FTC-happy.

### Template D: paid post offer (nano, $20–40)

> Your followers clearly love [Dog]. Your [recent video] did [X]K views on a [Y]K account 👏 Would you be up for one paid post this week?
>
> **What:** 1 TikTok or Reel (15–45s) of you seeing [Dog]'s portrait for the first time (the reveal reaction), plus 1 story with your link.
> **When:** live by [Fri Oct 16 / Sat Oct 17].
> **Pay:** **$[30]** via PayPal the day it goes live, plus your free print, plus 15% commission on every order through your link.

>
> Sound good? If your usual rate is different, tell me. I'd rather hear it than lowball you.

*Budget rule: $30 is the default. Go to $40 only for an account averaging more than 5K views. Above $40, offer gift + affiliate instead; don't haggle.*

### Template E: memorial variant (extra gentle)

Use **only** when the creator openly honors a dog who passed (in their bio or a pinned post) **and** it isn't recent (about 3+ months). Use the **Old Master, Watercolor or Botanical** style. **No pitch in this message, ever.**

> Hi [Name]. We're Pup Prints, a small portrait studio. We saw how much [Late dog] means to you, and we hope it's okay that we made this to honor [him/her]:
>
> [reveal link]
>
> No need to reply. It's simply yours. If anything about it doesn't feel right, tell us and we'll gently change it or take it down. 🤍

Only if they reply warmly, follow with: "Would it mean something to have it printed? We'd be honored to send it." Commerce comes up only if *they* ask.

### Template F: brand / UGC licensing ask

> [Name], the way [Dog] reacted to [his/her] portrait is honestly the best thing we've seen all week 😂 Would you let us share your video on @pupprints?
>
> • **Organic repost:** credited to you, free, anytime you say stop.
> • **Ads (optional):** we'd pay **$[25–75]** for 30 days of running it as an ad (Spark code or partnership ad). You can stop it any time after that.
>
> Either way, just reply "yes to repost" and/or "yes to ads" so we have it in writing. Thank you!!

## 5. What to say about AI (if asked)

The one-liner, word for word:
> **"Our AI artist creates it, and our team finishes and checks every portrait by hand before anything is printed."**

**If they push:**

| They ask | Say |
|---|---|
| "So it's AI art?" | "Yes, it starts with our AI artist, trained on classic painting styles. A person on our team checks every portrait for likeness, and we fix or remake anything that isn't right." |
| "Is it hand-painted?" | "No, and we'll never claim it is. That's also why we can show you your dog free in 30 seconds instead of making you pay and wait a week." |
| "I don't support AI art" | "Totally fair, thanks for being honest. We won't bug you again 💛" Then stop. |

**Never say:** "hand-painted," "painted by our artists," "100% human-made," or anything that hides the AI step.

## 7. FTC disclosure, in plain English

This is the law (FTC Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR Part 255), and our partner terms require it.

1. **If we gave them anything, they must say so.** That includes a free portrait, cash, a commission or a discount.
2. **In the content itself**, where people can't miss it:
   - in the first line of the caption, not buried in hashtags or behind "more";
   - **on screen and out loud in videos**;
   - repeated during lives.
3. **Words that work:**
   - **"#ad"**
   - **"Paid partnership with @pupprints"**
   - **"Gifted by @pupprints"**, but only if the free portrait was *all* they got
   - **"I earn a commission"** or "paid link" next to an affiliate link
4. **Words that don't work on their own:** "#sp", "#collab", "#partner", "#ambassador", "thanks @pupprints", or a disclosure only in the bio.
5. **Turn on the platform tool too:** Instagram "Paid partnership" label, TikTok "Content disclosure" (branded content) toggle, YouTube "Includes paid promotion." The FTC says the label alone isn't enough, so they need both.
6. **Honest claims only.**
   - They can't call it hand-painted.
   - They can't promise results we don't offer.
   - They can't post about it without actually having seen it.
7. **Our job:**
   - Brief them on all of this before they post.
   - **Check every partner post within 24 hours.**
   - Ask them to fix a missing disclosure right away. If they won't, stop the partnership.
8. **Never present creator posts as customer reviews**, and never ask for a positive review in exchange for anything (FTC Consumer Review Rule, 16 CFR 465).
9. **AI label:** if their video shows the portrait being generated, ask them to switch on TikTok's AI-generated content label.

## 8. Ops: tracking, codes, payouts

**Partner records** live in Admin → **Partners**. [prospects.csv](prospects.csv) imports through **Partners → Import**; the columns match exactly.

**Status ladder:**
`prospect` → `contacted` → `replied` → `negotiating` → **`gifted`** / **`active`** (`paused` / `declined`)

- The 15% link discount **only works** once status is `gifted` or `active`.
- Clicks start counting from `contacted`.

**Creating a partner:**
1. Partner page → **Generate code** (e.g. `RUFUS15`). Set commission: 0.15, or 0.20 for Founding Partners.
2. **Copy link** gives their share link: `https://pup-prints.vercel.app/?ref=RUFUS15`. The discount applies automatically at checkout, and attribution lasts 30 days from the click.
3. **Copy stats link** gives their private page with live clicks, orders and commission.

**Important: typed codes don't work out of the box.**
- Partner codes are applied through the link, not typed at checkout. If a creator says "use code RUFUS15" in a video, viewers who type it will find it doesn't exist in Stripe.
- **Fix:** tell creators to say "link in bio." If someone insists on a spoken code, create a Stripe promotion code with the same name on the `PP_PARTNER_15` coupon (Stripe → Coupons).
- **Typed-code orders aren't auto-attributed.** Each Friday, filter Stripe payments by promotion code and add those commissions by hand.

**Gift prints** (max 5 this week; a 12×16 fine-art print costs about $16 landed). Run them **through our own site** so the print file is upscaled properly and goes through QA and Printful like any order:
1. One-time Stripe setup:
   - Coupon `INTERNAL100`: 100% off, duration "Once".
   - Per gift, add a **single-use** promotion code, e.g. `GIFT-RUFUS` (max redemptions 1).
   - Stripe supports $0 "no-cost" Checkout orders, and our webhook treats them as paid.
2. Open the site in a **private window**, with no `?ref=` link, so the code box shows.
3. Upload the creator's dog photo at `/create` → same style → 12×16 fine-art print.
4. At checkout, enter **the creator's shipping address and *your* email**, so they don't get our customer emails, and apply `GIFT-RUFUS`.
5. Log the ~$16 cost in the war-plan tracking table. Add a note on their partner record.

The Gift Studio image is web-size (for the reveal page), so **don't** send it to Printful directly; it's too small to print sharply. Prints arrive in about 5–9 business days. That makes the **unboxing** week-2/3 content; the **digital reveal reaction** is this week's content.

**Paid posts:**
- Pay on the day the post goes live, via PayPal **Goods & Services** (buyer protection), and keep the receipt.
- Collect a W-9 before cumulative payments pass $600. 1099 reporting only applies above $2,000 in 2026, but a W-9 on file keeps you clean.

**Commission payouts:**
- Commissions **lock 30 days after delivery** (the love-it guarantee window).
- They're paid **monthly on the 15th via PayPal** (Wise if needed).
- Refunded orders don't earn.
- The full terms are in [partner-agreement.md](partner-agreement.md).

## 10. Do-not list

1. **No spam.**
   - No copy-paste DMs without a real gift and a specific detail.
   - No mass-tagging.
   - No comment-section pitches.
   - No follow/unfollow.
2. **Respect "no" the first time.** Mark them declined. Never re-pitch under another account.
3. **Never post, repost or advertise a creator's dog (or their photo) without a written yes.** Screenshot the yes into their partner notes.
4. **Never cold-pitch grief.**
   - Do-not-contact: **@tuckerbudzyn** (Tucker died 2026-09-02) and **@monienature** (loss in June 2026).
   - Handle only with opt-in care: @wolfgang2242, @ambertheseniorrescuedchi, @the_doggie_days, @booplethesnoot, @knoxandbear.
   - Skip Manny the Frenchie (died 2023) and any account whose last posts mention a loss.
5. **No minors.**
   - Don't contract or pay accounts run by minors.
   - Where kids appear on camera (e.g. @minidoodlebentley), check the Illinois, California and Minnesota child-creator rules before any paid deal.
6. **No trademarked characters or franchises** in any portrait or brief.
7. **No "hand-painted," no fake urgency, no invented numbers** in anything we send.
8. **No buying followers, views or comments.** That's illegal under 16 CFR 465 and ruins the data.
9. **Don't DM from your personal account** unless the creator already knows you. Use the brand.
10. **Don't send a portrait you wouldn't frame yourself.**
