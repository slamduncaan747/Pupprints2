# Pup Prints Creator & Affiliate Agreement (template)

> **This is a template, not legal advice.** It's written in plain English for a small US business. Have a lawyer review it before relying on it, especially for paid deals over a few hundred dollars, usage rights for ads, or creators outside the US.
>
> **How to use it this week:**
> - Paste it into an email or a Google Doc.
> - Fill in the brackets.
> - Get a reply that says **"I agree"**, or an e-signature (free: Dropbox Sign, DocuSign Free, or a Google Form with a checkbox).
> - Save the reply in the partner's notes in Admin.

**Between:** Pup Prints ("**we**", "**us**"), [legal entity name, state], contact: partners@[domain]
**And:** [Creator legal name] ("**you**"), handle(s): [@handle on platforms], email: [email], PayPal: [paypal email]
**Start date:** [date]

## 1. What this is

You're joining the Pup Prints creator and affiliate program. You may share Pup Prints with your audience. You earn commission on orders you refer. If we've agreed on a paid post (section 4), you'll deliver it and we'll pay you for it. You're an independent creator, not our employee, agent or exclusive partner.

## 2. Your link, your code, the discount

- We'll give you a personal link (`https://pup-prints.vercel.app/?ref=[CODE]`, or the same path on our future domain) and a code name.
- **Your audience discount:** [15]% off their first order through your link. It applies automatically at checkout. Discounts don't stack with other codes or promotions.
- **Attribution window (cookie): 30 days.** An order counts as yours if the customer clicked your link within the 30 days before buying, on the same device and browser. If they clicked several partner links, the **most recent** partner link clicked wins. Orders placed with your code typed in at checkout also count, where we've set one up for you.
- We'll give you a private stats page showing clicks, orders and commission.

## 3. Commission

- **Rate:** [15]% ([20% Founding Partner rate, if you're one of our first 25 creators to post by Dec 8, 2026]) of the **net amount** the customer paid for the product. "Net" means after discounts, and excluding sales tax, shipping charges (if any), tips and refunded amounts.
- **No commission on:**
  - your own orders;
  - orders we refund or remake under our love-it guarantee;
  - chargebacks;
  - fraudulent or test orders;
  - orders that came through coupon or deal sites, or through paid search on our brand name.
- **Locking:** commission locks **30 days after the order is delivered** (or 30 days after purchase for digital-only orders). That's our refund window.
- **Payment:** locked commission is paid **monthly on the 15th via PayPal** (or Wise if PayPal isn't available). [No minimum payout / Minimum payout $10; smaller balances roll over.]
- **Taxes:** US creators provide a **W-9** before the first payout. We'll issue tax forms where the law requires it. You're responsible for your own taxes.
- **Statements:** your stats page is the running statement. If something looks wrong, tell us within 60 days and we'll check it together.

## 5. Disclosure and honesty (required, it's the law)

You agree to follow the FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) and each platform's branded-content rules:

1. **Disclose clearly in every post that mentions us**:
   - "#ad", "Paid partnership with @pupprints", or "Gifted by @pupprints" (the last only if the free portrait is all you received);
   - "I earn a commission" or "paid link" next to affiliate links.
2. **Put it where it can't be missed:**
   - at the start of captions, not in a hashtag pile or only in your bio;
   - **spoken and on screen in videos**;
   - repeated during lives.
3. **Use the platform's tool too:** Instagram Paid Partnership label, TikTok Content Disclosure, YouTube paid-promotion box.
4. **Describe the product truthfully.**
   - Portraits are created by our AI artist and finished and checked by our team. Never call them hand-painted.
   - Don't make claims about price, delivery, results or guarantees beyond what's on our website.
   - Turn on TikTok's AI-generated content label if you show the portrait being generated.
5. **Don't present your content as a customer review**, and don't post reviews of us on review sites unless you clearly disclose our relationship.

If a post is missing a disclosure, we'll ask you to fix it. If it's not fixed within 48 hours, we may end this agreement and withhold the paid-post fee for that post.

## 6. Things you agree not to do

- Post your link or code on coupon, deal or cashback sites, or in spam, mass DMs or comment chains.
- Run paid ads using our name, or bid on "Pup Prints" (or misspellings) in search ads, without our written OK.
- Use trademarked characters or franchises (e.g. Disney, Star Wars, sports teams) in content featuring us.
- Use our logo or name in a way that suggests you're us.
- Buy followers, views or engagement.
- Contract with us if you're under 18. A parent or guardian must sign for any account run by a minor.

## 7. Content and usage rights

- **Your content stays yours.**
- **Organic reshare:** you give us a free, non-exclusive license to reshare your posts about us on our own social accounts and website, **always credited** to you. Stop it any time by telling us; we'll remove reshares within 7 days.
- **Ads:** we only run your content as an ad (Spark Ads, partnership ads, whitelisting) if section 4 says so, or you agree in writing. The term and fee go there.
- **Your dog's portrait:** we created it for you, and you may use it personally and in your content however you like. We won't publish your dog's portrait or photo in our marketing without your written OK. We may keep a private copy for order history and quality records.
- **Our materials:** you may use the logos, images and copy we send you only to promote Pup Prints while this agreement is active.

## 8. Our promises to you

- Pay what's owed, on time, with a clear statement.
- Answer your questions within 1 business day.
- Honor the love-it guarantee for your audience. If a follower has a problem, we fix it.
- Never ask you to say anything untrue.

## 9. Ending the agreement

- **Either side** can end this at any time, for any reason, by email or DM.
- **After it ends:**
  - your link stops earning for clicks after the end date;
  - orders from earlier clicks (within the 30-day window) still earn and are paid on the normal schedule;
  - you remove unpublished drafts, and we stop resharing your content within 7 days;
  - ad rights end on the date in section 4, or immediately if we end the agreement for a breach.
- We may end it immediately if you seriously break sections 5 or 6. Commission from fraudulent or prohibited orders isn't paid.

## 10. The small print

- **Changes:** we may update the program terms with 14 days' notice by email. Changes never reduce commission already earned.
- **Confidentiality:** keep our non-public info (e.g. rates and stats) private, and we'll do the same for yours.
- **Liability:** neither side is liable to the other for indirect losses. Our total liability under this agreement is limited to the commissions and fees owed to you.
- **Law:** this agreement is governed by the laws of [State], USA.
- **Whole agreement:** this document plus any written deal terms in section 4 is the whole agreement.
