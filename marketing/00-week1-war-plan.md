## 0. The honest math

**What "profit" means here:** revenue minus product, shipping, Stripe and AI costs, minus creator commissions, minus the $100 of paid spend, minus the cost of any prints we gift. Founder time, sample orders for QA, domain and software are not counted.

**Profit per order** (from `docs/research/fulfillment.md`, with the 15% launch code applied):

| Product | Price | With the 15% code | Gross profit (no code) | Gross profit (with code) | With code + 15% creator commission |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Canvas 16×20 (hero)** | $99 | $84.15 | $55 | **~$42** | ~$29 |
| Framed 12×16 | $95 | $80.75 | $47 | ~$34 | ~$22 |
| Fine-art print 12×16 | $45 | $38.25 | $26 | ~$19 | ~$13 |
| Digital file only | $29 | $24.65 | $26 | ~$23 | ~$19 |

**So the target is 30–35 orders, not 20–25.** Most early orders will use a code: the 15% launch code, a 15% creator link, or WELCOME10 at 10%. That pulls the blended profit down to about $36–38 per order.

| Line | Base case | Goal case |
|---|---|---|
| Orders | 15 | **33** |
| Mix | 8 canvas, 3 framed, 2 print, 2 digital | 16 canvas, 7 framed, 5 print, 5 digital |
| Gross profit (about 3 in 4 orders use a 15% code) | ~$565 | ~$1,210 |
| Add-on bumps (ornament $19 / mug $25, roughly +$10–14 profit each, on ~1 in 4 orders) | +$40 | +$100 |
| Creator commissions (~1 order in 5) | −$30 | −$75 |
| Paid spend | −$100 | −$100 |
| Gifted prints for creators (max 5 × ~$16) | −$48 | −$80 |
| Remake/refund reserve (3% of revenue) | −$40 | −$85 |
| **Profit** | **≈ $390** | **≈ $970** (one more canvas gets you to $1,000) |

**Levers that raise profit per order without fake discounts:**
- Default to the **16×20 canvas** (it's already preselected).
- Mention the **ornament or mug add-on** in every personal follow-up ("want [Dog] on an ornament too? +$19").
- Offer **two-dog portraits** to multi-dog homes.

**How likely is $1,000?** Not very. With zero reviews, zero followers and $100, our honest odds of $1,000 profit this week are about 1 in 4. The base case is 12–18 orders and $300–600 profit. Two things change the odds:
1. **The warm network.** People who already know you, seeing *their own dog* as a portrait, buy at 15–30%. Strangers buy at 1–2%.
2. **One creator post or one of our videos taking off.** We can't plan for that, but we can buy more lottery tickets: 60+ gift DMs and 18 videos this week.

The plan puts most of the hours where cash arrives fastest. It also seeds the creator pipeline that pays off in Q4 (Nov 16 – Dec 10) even if this week falls short.

**One decision is already made: no fake urgency.** We use the real Founding 100 code (below), personal asks and the gift moment. No countdown timers, no "only 3 left", and no follower or review numbers we don't have.

## 3. The launch offer (honest, real cap, real deadline)

**Founding 100: 15% off for our first 100 customers.**
- Code: **`FOUNDING100`**. 15% off, one use per customer.
- **Hard cap: 100 redemptions. Hard expiry: Sat Oct 31, 11:59pm PT.** Whichever comes first. Never extend it, and never restart it under a new name.
- **Set it up in Stripe** (5 minutes):
  1. Products → Coupons → New: 15%, duration "Once".
  2. Add promotion code `FOUNDING100`.
  3. Limit to 100 redemptions, set the expiry date, and tick "first-time order only".
- **Post the real count** in stories ("31 of 100 claimed"). Read it from Stripe's redemption count. That is honest scarcity.
- **Codes don't stack.** Checkout takes one code: Founding, WELCOME10 or a creator link.
- **Creator links apply their 15% automatically** and hide the code box, so creator buyers never need FOUNDING100.

**Trust signals to lead with instead of reviews:**
- "See it free before you pay."
- "Love it or we remake or refund it."
- "Free US shipping."
- "Hi-res file included free."
- "Every print checked by a person."
- "Send the Gift Reveal today; the print follows."

**Launch framing:** "Be one of our first 100." It's true, and it turns zero reviews into an invitation.

## 8. Rules of the week

1. **Speed over polish.** A good portrait sent today beats a perfect one sent Thursday.
2. **Reply to every comment and DM within 30 minutes, 8am–10pm.** In week 1, replies *are* the conversion rate.
3. **Never post a creator's or friend's dog publicly without a clear "yes"** (screenshot it).
4. **Disclose everything:**
   - Creators use #ad, "Paid partnership" or "Gifted by @pupprints" *in the content itself*.
   - When asked how the art is made, the answer is: "our AI artist creates it, our team finishes and checks it."
   - Never say "hand-painted."
5. **No fabricated proof.**
   - No made-up reviews, counts or "as seen on."
   - Never reuse creator posts as "customer reviews."
6. **Memorial accounts get the gentle template or nothing.** Never pitch an account that has recently lost a dog. The do-not-contact list is in the [playbook](creators/outreach-playbook.md#10-do-not-list).
7. **Stop-loss:**
   - If Thursday ends below 8 orders, pull the dog-park blitz forward to Friday.
   - Double the warm-network second ring.
   - Put the full $40 into a 3rd nano post instead of the boost.
