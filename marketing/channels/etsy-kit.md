# Etsy Kit: shop setup + 5 ready-to-paste listings

**Why Etsy:** buyers already search "custom dog portrait" there every day, especially in Q4.

**Why it's #9 on this week's list:** new shops have no reviews and rank slowly, so expect 0–2 Etsy orders this week. The real payoff is Nov–Dec.

**Setup time:** about 90 minutes on Wed Oct 14.

> **Policy check (done 2026-10-13):** Etsy's own pages block automated reads, so this is verified through Etsy's House Rules text as indexed by search, plus TechCrunch's coverage of the Creativity Standards (Jul 2024), which are still in force. **Confirm the exact checkboxes in Shop Manager when you list.**
> - **AI-made art is allowed** on Etsy under the **"Designed by [seller]"** category, as long as it comes from the seller's own original prompts or designs.
> - Sellers **must disclose in the listing description** that the item was created with AI.
> - Items printed by someone else need a **production partner** listed: Printful.
> - Selling AI *prompts* is banned. We sell finished art, so that's fine.
> - Some 2026 seller guides mention an extra "AI" checkbox in the listing form. If you see it, tick it.

## 4. Fulfilling Etsy orders through our pipeline

Etsy orders don't touch our Stripe checkout, so run them through the site as **$0 internal orders**. That way the print file is upscaled, QA'd and sent to Printful exactly like a site order.

| Step | Do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Etsy app notification → **thank the buyer** in Messages and ask for 1–3 photos (template below). | 1 min |
| 2 | Photo arrives → open a **private browser window** (no `?ref=` link) → `/create` → upload the photo → the buyer's style → generate → **QA** (markings, eyes, ears, no artifacts). **Keep that tab open**, because the portrait you approve is the exact one that gets printed. | 3 min |
| 3 | Send the **proof image** (screenshot or download) in Etsy Messages: "Here's [Dog]! Reply 'approved' or tell us anything to change." | 1 min |
| 4 | On "approved": in that same tab → choose the Etsy product and size → checkout with **the buyer's shipping address**, **your email**, and a single-use 100%-off code (Stripe coupon `INTERNAL100` → promotion code `ETSY-[etsy order #]`, max 1 use). If they asked for changes, regenerate in the tab first and send a new proof. | 5 min |
| 5 | The order appears in Admin → Orders → it flows to print QA → Printful as usual. Add a note: "Etsy #[number]". | 1 min |
| 6 | When it ships, copy the tracking from Admin or Printful → Etsy → **Mark as shipped** with tracking. That's important for the shop's ranking and payouts. | 2 min |
| 7 | **Hi-res file:** download it from the order (Admin → Orders → order → digital file) → send it in Etsy Messages. | 1 min |
| 8 | Day after delivery: a short thank-you + "If you love it, a review helps a tiny shop more than you'd think." **No incentive for reviews** (Etsy and FTC rules). | 1 min |

**Message templates (Etsy):**
- **After purchase:** "Thank you so much! 🐾 Please reply with 1–3 clear photos of [Dog], eyes visible and natural light if possible. We'll send your proof within 24 hours (usually sooner)."
- **Free preview before purchase:** "We'd love to! Send a photo of your dog and the style you're curious about, and we'll send you a free preview here, no obligation."

**Etsy money timing:** new shops often get a **payment reserve** (funds released after delivery or after a set number of days). An Etsy sale this week is real profit, but the **cash may arrive later**. Count it in the profit tracker on the order date and note "Etsy, pending".
