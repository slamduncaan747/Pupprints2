# Shelter Partnership: free portraits for adoptable dogs

**The idea:** we make free fine-art portraits of adoptable dogs for local shelters and rescues to use alongside their listings and on social.
- **The dogs** get a second look.
- **The shelter** gets scroll-stopping content for free.
- **We** get a real story for local press and content people share for the right reasons.

**Our cost:** about $0.06 per portrait plus about 3 minutes of your time.

**This is real help, not a stunt.** Keep it that way:
- No cause-marketing claims tied to sales.
- No using shelter dogs in ads without permission.
- **Never replace the real adoption photo.** Adopters need to see the actual dog.

---

## 1. The offer (what we say yes to)

| We give | Details |
|---|---|
| **Free digital portraits** | For every adoptable dog they send, or start with their **10 longest-stay dogs**. Turnaround within 48 hours. Hi-res files, cropped for listings (4:5) and stories (9:16). |
| **Style** | Royal or Admiral for personality; Watercolor or Old Master for gentle souls. |
| **A "portrait + real photo" pair image** | Portrait on the left, real photo on the right, so listings stay honest. |
| **Adoption-day print** *(from week 2, as budget allows)* | An 8×10 print of the dog's portrait for the adopting family, mailed to the shelter. About $15–20 each, so we cap it monthly. |
| **Content** | We feature their dogs (with permission) in our "Shelter Saturday" video series and tag them. |

| We ask | Why |
|---|---|
| Permission to use their listing photos to make the portraits | They own the photos |
| Written OK before we post any dog on our accounts | Brand safety |
| A tag or mention when they post the portraits (optional) | Reach, but never a condition |
| A quote from the director for press (only if they're comfortable) | Local news angle |

**What we don't do:**
- No "a portion of every purchase goes to…" claims. Several US states regulate those as "commercial co-ventures" with registration rules; check with a lawyer before any per-sale donation promise.
- If we donate money (e.g. on Giving Tuesday, Dec 1), it's a **flat donation we decide**, announced after it's made.

## 2. Process (week 1)

| When | Step | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Tue (Day 1)** | Pick 5 targets | Your county shelter + 4 local rescues. Find them on Petfinder or Adopt-a-Pet ("dogs near [ZIP]") and on Instagram. Smaller rescues answer faster. Also on our list: @psar_rachael (PawSafe Animal Rescue, CT; info@pawsafe.org) and, for later, @whitneysdogs (Hearts & Bones Rescue, Dallas). |
| Tue | Send the email (§3) | To the volunteer, marketing or foster coordinator if listed; otherwise the general inbox. Attach **2 sample portraits** made from their *public* listing photos as a private demo. Don't post them. |
| **Thu** | Call | Use the phone script (§4). Ask for 10 minutes. |
| Thu–Fri | First yes → 10 portraits within 24h | Make them in Admin → Gifts. Name files `[shelter]-[dog]-[style].jpg`. Deliver in a shared Google Drive folder, plus the pair images. |
| Fri–Sat | Content (with written OK) | Scripts **#16** and **#17** ([30-video-scripts](../content/30-video-scripts.md)). Tag the shelter. Caption: "Juno is looking for a home at [Shelter]." Never push our sale in the same post. |
| Mon Oct 19 | Press pitch (§6) | Only after: the shelter has approved, ≥10 portraits exist, and a director quote has been approved. |
| Ongoing | Monthly batch | A new batch every month. Adoption-day prints from November. Giving Tuesday (Dec 1) feature. |

**Tracking:**
- Add each shelter in Admin → Partners: platform `facebook` or `instagram`, niche `shelter`, status `active`, commission 0%.
- Use `utm_source=shelter_[name]` on any link they share.
- Note adoptions they report. Only publish adoption numbers the shelter confirms.
